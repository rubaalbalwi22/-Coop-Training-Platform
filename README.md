Coop Training Platform 🎓

A mobile application designed to help university students find and manage cooperative training opportunities in one centralized platform.

📱 About the Project

The Coop Training Platform was developed as a graduation project to simplify the cooperative training process for university students.

The platform helps students discover suitable training opportunities, submit their CVs, and manage their training experience.

✨ Features

* 🔎 Search for cooperative training opportunities
* 📄 Submit and manage CVs
* 🎯 Filter opportunities based on major
* ⭐ Receive feedback and ratings after training
* 📱 User-friendly mobile interface
